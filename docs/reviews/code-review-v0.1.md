# spendgate v0.1 code review (pre-release tree)

## Summary and verdict

**Verdict: Fail. Not ready to tag.** Three high-severity defects are confirmed on money paths.

The overall design holds up. The ledger flows are state-guarded and idempotent. Rounding goes up everywhere. There is no float money and no `Any` in `core/` or `ledger/`. The hold is shielded against cancellation, and every gate passes. But the pipeline relies on two assumptions that are not true:

1. **Provider adapters never raise.** If an adapter's `usage()` or `error()` raises after the request was sent, the call is released at cost 0. I showed this end to end through a real adapter (Google, a 5xx with a malformed `Retry-After`).
2. **The sweeper is the backstop for a hold the owner failed to close.** The owner's own heartbeat renews every pending lease of its instance. While the process lives, an abandoned hold is never expired, never charged, and never released.

Separately, library mode's heartbeat bypasses the pipeline's lease bookkeeping. As a result, every stream longer than about 40 s is cut and charged.

Every finding below was confirmed by reading the code. Findings marked "probe" were also reproduced with throwaway scripts that drive the existing test harness (`tests/integration/harness.py`); no repository file was modified.

Checked and found sound: the hold is shielded, and a cancel during the hold releases at 0 (`pipeline.py:194-222`). Settle and release are state-guarded and idempotent (`sql.py:92-99`), so `_book` retries cannot double-book. Late settle books only the overrun. Holds are all-or-nothing across budgets with ordered locks. Postgres retries only rolled-back conflicts and never retries after an ambiguous COMMIT. Failover happens only after `not_processed`. `transport.classify` defaults unknown exceptions to "sent". Stream endings all settle once through a shielded task, and `RelayResponse` always closes the body. No upstream contact happens without a `Held` (the only `Forwarder.run` call site is after the hold). Money is integer nano with ceiling rounding throughout `core/money.py` and `core/cost.py`.
